A circuit is this model with something learned removed from it — either a binary mask multiplied into its weights, or a set of residual-stream edges cut by hooks around the forward pass. The density is the share of that circuit's own unit left open, which the badge names: 26% of edges is not 26% of weights. The answer is the first token, which is what a circuit's first token score is measured on; anything dimmed after it is the sample carrying on, and a pruned circuit usually cannot stop — losing the newline that ends the frame is not the same as getting the word wrong. The API behind this page: GET /health, GET /circuits, POST /infer (task names the frame; omit it to send whole prompts).
